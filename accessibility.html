Accessibility Statement

Last updated: August 2026

Baile Mór Strategy Group Inc. is committed to providing a website that is accessible, inclusive, and usable for all visitors.

We strive to ensure that our digital content is accessible to individuals with disabilities and are committed to continuous improvement in accessibility practices. Our goal is to align the website with generally recognized accessibility standards and best practices, including the principles of the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG).

Accessibility Features

We endeavour to:

  • Use clear and consistent navigation.
  • Provide meaningful page structure and headings.
  • Support keyboard navigation where practical.
  • Use readable fonts and sufficient colour contrast.
  • Provide alternative text descriptions for images where appropriate.
  • Design content that can be accessed across a variety of devices and assistive technologies.

Ongoing Improvement

Accessibility is an ongoing process. We periodically review website content and functionality to identify opportunities for improvement.

Feedback and Alternative Formats

If you experience difficulty accessing any content on this website or require information in an alternative format, please contact us. We welcome feedback and will make reasonable efforts to address accessibility concerns promptly.
