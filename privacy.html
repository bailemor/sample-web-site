Last updated: August 2026

We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide, including:

  • Information included in correspondence with us.

We may also collect limited technical and usage information through the website or analytics services, which may include:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) address.
  • Browser type and device information.

We may use personal information to:

This website may use cookies or similar technologies to support functionality, maintain security, and understand website usage. You may adjust your browser settings to block or delete cookies. If cookies are disabled, some website features may not function as intended.

6. Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, meet legitimate business needs, or comply with legal obligations. Information that is no longer required is securely deleted, destroyed, or anonymized, as appropriate.

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to personal information we hold about you, ask that inaccurate information be corrected, or inquire about how your information has been used or disclosed. We may need to verify your identity before responding to a request.
