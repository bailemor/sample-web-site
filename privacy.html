Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Baile Mór Strategy Group Inc. ("Baile Mór", "we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting personal information entrusted to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, retain, and safeguard personal information obtained through this website.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide, including:

  • Name and organization.
  • Email address and telephone number.
  • Information submitted through contact forms.
  • Information included in correspondence with us.

We may also collect limited technical and usage information through the website or analytics services, which may include:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) address.
  • Browser type and device information.
  • Pages visited and time spent on pages.
  • Referring website or source.

2. How We Use Information

We may use personal information to:

  • Respond to inquiries and requests.
  • Communicate regarding our services.
  • Operate, administer, secure, and improve the website.
  • Understand website usage and user experience.
  • Comply with applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

We do not sell personal information to third parties.

3. Cookies and Analytics

This website may use cookies or similar technologies to support functionality, maintain security, and understand website usage. We use two analytics services, described below.

Cloudflare Web Analytics

Our website host, Cloudflare, Inc., provides Cloudflare Web Analytics. It tells us how many visits the website receives, which pages are viewed, which websites referred visitors, visitors' countries, browser and device types, and how quickly pages load. Cloudflare states that this service does not use cookies or similar browser storage and does not track individuals over time. Because it does not use cookies, it runs for every visitor.

LinkedIn Insight Tag

With your permission, this website uses the LinkedIn Insight Tag, provided by LinkedIn. LinkedIn uses it to give us combined reports about visitors who are LinkedIn members, such as job titles, industries, seniority and company size. These reports do not identify individual visitors to us. To produce them, the tag sets cookies in your browser and sends LinkedIn information such as the page address, the referring page, your IP address, browser and device information, and the time of your visit. LinkedIn may process this information outside Canada. LinkedIn's handling of this information is governed by the LinkedIn Privacy Policy and LinkedIn Cookie Policy.

The LinkedIn Insight Tag is off by default. It loads only if you select "Accept" on the cookie notice. If you select "Decline", it does not load.

Your choices

You can change your choice at any time by selecting "Cookie settings" at the bottom of any page. Your choice is saved in your browser for 12 months, and we then ask again. You may also adjust your browser settings to block or delete cookies, including cookies LinkedIn has already set. If cookies are disabled, some website features may not function as intended.

4. Disclosure of Information

We may disclose personal information to service providers that support website hosting, analytics, communications, or other business operations; where required or permitted by law; or where reasonably necessary to protect legal rights, safety, or security. We expect service providers to use appropriate safeguards and process information only for authorized purposes.

5. Safeguards

Baile Mór uses reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, use, disclosure, alteration, loss, or destruction. No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

6. Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, meet legitimate business needs, or comply with legal obligations. Information that is no longer required is securely deleted, destroyed, or anonymized, as appropriate.

7. Access and Correction

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to personal information we hold about you, ask that inaccurate information be corrected, or inquire about how your information has been used or disclosed. We may need to verify your identity before responding to a request.

8. Third-Party Websites and Services

This website may contain links to third-party websites or use third-party services. Their collection and handling of personal information are governed by their own privacy policies. Baile Mór is not responsible for the privacy practices of third parties.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be posted on this page together with a revised "Last updated" date.
