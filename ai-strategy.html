AI Strategy

AI doesn’t need more hype. It needs governance, judgment, and a plan grounded in how the public sector actually works.

Every municipal and institutional leader is asking the same question: what should we be doing about AI? The real risk for public-sector organizations isn’t the technology — it’s adopting it without the governance, risk management, and change-management discipline that public accountability demands.

Baile Mór’s AI Strategy practice is built on that distinction. Rather than a technical AI vendor, we bring 30+ years of municipal and public-sector leadership experience — understanding how Councils, Boards, and administrations decide, manage risk, and are held accountable — combined with formal training through McMaster DeGroote’s AI Strategy Certificate (Fall 2026) and extensive Big Four AI governance and enterprise strategy programs. This pairing helps organizations find where AI delivers real value — efficient service delivery, faster back-office processes, better use of scarce staff time — built on a governance-first strategy Council, staff, and community can trust.
