AI Strategy

Governance-first AI strategy for the public sector: where AI delivers real value, and how to adopt it with the risk management public accountability demands.

AI doesn’t need more hype. It needs governance, judgment, and a plan grounded in how the public sector actually works. We help organizations find where AI delivers real value, such as efficient service delivery, faster back-office processes, and better use of scarce staff time, built on a governance-first strategy Council, staff, and community can trust.

Every municipal and institutional leader is asking what they should be doing about AI. The real risk for public-sector organizations isn’t the technology. It’s adopting it without the governance, risk management, and change-management discipline that public accountability demands.

Rather than a technical AI vendor, we bring 30+ years of municipal and public-sector leadership experience, understanding how Councils, Boards, and administrations decide, manage risk, and are held accountable. That experience is combined with formal training through McMaster DeGroote’s AI Strategy Certificate (Fall 2026) and extensive Big Four AI governance and enterprise strategy programs.

The Baile Mór AI Framework

Five phases, right-sized, from first assessment to sustained value. A posture diagnostic decides where each client starts and how far each phase goes.

  1. Phase 1

    Readiness Assessment

    Strategy

    Check data, legacy systems and staff capacity before any tool is chosen.

    Output: maturity baseline, 12-month target, posture score

  2. Phase 2

    Governance Baseline

    Governance

    Confirm posture with leadership; set where AI is allowed and who signs off.

    Output: one-page AI checklist, use policy, named owner

  3. Phase 3

    Targeted Pilot

    Implementation

    One workflow, one measured outcome: hours saved, cost avoided or service level.

    Output: baseline, pilot evaluation, scale-or-stop decision

  4. Phase 4

    AI Fluency

    Implementation

    Role-based training for Council, leaders, staff and faculty, run alongside the pilot.

    Output: Council diligence briefing, staff confidence measured

  5. Phase 5

    Report, Scale and Sustain

    Strategy

    Report results in cost and service terms; scale only what worked.

    Output: results report, multi-year roadmap, benefits tracking, AI risk management model

Risk management

Runs through every phase: a one-page risk check at each gate, privacy and bias reviews, exit-ready procurement terms, and a risk register that grows with AI use.
