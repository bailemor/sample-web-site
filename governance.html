Governance

Difficult decisions deserve independent, experienced counsel.

Councils, Boards, and executive teams are regularly asked to navigate governance questions with no easy precedent — restructuring an agency, resolving a shareholder relationship, or deciding whether a service belongs inside or outside the organization. These decisions carry legal, financial, and reputational weight, and getting independent, senior-level advice matters.

Baile Mór provides trusted governance and strategic leadership advisory to elected officials, boards, and senior executives — helping organizations clarify accountability, strengthen decision-making, and design governance structures built to last.

Representative work

  • London Transit Commission

    Delivered an independent governance review.

  • City of Windsor

    Conducted a governance review of the city’s Agencies, Boards & Commissions, realigning more than 65 ABCs with Council’s strategic direction and reducing the number of advisory committees.
