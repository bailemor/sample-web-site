Our Work

Our Work

Big ideas, applied with practical rigour, in service of stronger, more resilient communities.

Financial Strategy

  • City of Cornwall

    Developed a 10-year Long-Term Financial Plan and evergreen forecasting model addressing capital, staffing, revenue, debt, and reserve requirements.

  • Cape Breton Regional Municipality

    Led an independent financial-management and performance review on behalf of the Nova Scotia Department of Municipal Affairs.

Governance

  • London Transit Commission

    Delivered an independent governance review.

  • City of Windsor

    Conducted a governance review of the city’s Agencies, Boards & Commissions, realigning more than 65 ABCs with Council’s strategic direction and reducing the number of advisory committees.

Operational Improvement

  • City of Waterloo

    Led an organizational review spanning the original “2013 Forward” restructuring initiative, built around aligning the City’s structure with community needs, operational efficiency, appropriate staffing, and organizational culture.

  • Waterloo Region, Durham Region, and City of Guelph

    Conducted service delivery reviews identifying opportunities for improved service delivery and cost reduction across municipal programs and operations.
