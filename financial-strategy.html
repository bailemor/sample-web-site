Financial Strategy

When today’s budget can’t carry tomorrow’s obligations, you need a plan — not a guess.

Rising service demands, aging infrastructure, and growth pressures are outpacing traditional revenue tools across Canadian municipalities and public institutions. Boards and Councils don’t need more forecasts — they need a financial strategy that connects service levels, infrastructure, growth, taxation, reserves, and debt into one coherent, decision-ready plan.

At Baile Mór, financial strategy isn’t a spreadsheet exercise — it’s the discipline of turning fiscal pressure into a defensible, Council-ready roadmap. We build long-term financial plans, fiscal sustainability reviews, reserve strategies, and evergreen forecasting models that stand up to scrutiny and support real decisions.

Representative work

  • City of Cornwall

    Developed a 10-year Long-Term Financial Plan and evergreen forecasting model addressing capital, staffing, revenue, debt, and reserve requirements.

  • Cape Breton Regional Municipality

    Led an independent financial-management and performance review on behalf of the Nova Scotia Department of Municipal Affairs.
