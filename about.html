About

Bruce Peever, FCMC

Founder & Principal, Baile Mór Strategy Group

Great towns need big ideas.

Your community, council, or institution faces decisions that will shape the next decade, and deserves a strategic partner who has sat on both sides of the table, as client and consultant.

For more than 30 years, I’ve worked alongside municipal governments, universities, and public sector organizations across Canada, first as a Chief Administrative Officer, later as a Partner in the Public Sector Practice of a Big Four firm. Across hundreds of engagements, from small rural townships to Toronto, Ottawa, and Halifax, the goal has stayed the same: big ideas, applied with practical rigour, in service of stronger, more resilient communities.

You deserve the same calibre of strategic counsel as the country’s largest organizations, delivered personally, from start to finish. That’s why Baile Mór is lean, founder-led, and focused entirely on real answers. The name is Gaelic for “great town,” a reminder that impact comes from the boldness of the thinking, not the size of the firm.

Career at a glance

  • 35+years leading and advising the public sector
  • 9provinces, from British Columbia to Newfoundland and Labrador
  • 260+engagements with municipalities, universities, colleges, police services and federal agencies
  • 13years as a Chief Administrative Officer
  • 17years at a Big Four firm, Partner from 2019

Here’s how I can help

  • Financial Strategy

    Connecting service levels, infrastructure, growth, taxation, and reserves into a practical long-term plan, as I’ve done for cities, towns, and townships from Northern Ontario to Vancouver Island.

  • Governance

    Independent advice that strengthens accountability and decision-making, reflected in reviews for an Atlantic Canadian city, an economic development corporation, a large city’s transit commission, and numerous public agencies.

  • Operational Improvement

    Hundreds of engagements assessing structures and service models, delivering implementation-ready roadmaps, not reports that sit on a shelf.

  • AI Strategy

    Moving you from curiosity to practical value with governance frameworks and responsible AI policies that manage risk while capturing real benefit.

The work that matters is never about small fixes. It’s about asking the harder question and building something that lasts.

Great towns need big ideas.

I’d welcome the chance to bring mine to yours.
